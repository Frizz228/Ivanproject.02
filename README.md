# 🌟 Amongusik – Smart Fitness Band  

**Amongusik** is a modern smart fitness band website created by **Ivan Tulin** 👨‍💻.  
It showcases both a personal portfolio and a product landing page — clean, simple, and stylish.

---

## 🚀 Overview

Amongusik helps you stay on top of your health and daily activity.  
It’s **stylish**, **lightweight**, and **reliable** — the perfect companion for sports and leisure.

---

## 💓 Features

- 🩺 Heart rate and sleep monitoring  
- 💧 Water resistance up to 50 m  
- 🔋 Battery life up to 7 days*  
- ⚡ Wireless charging  

> *Battery life depends on usage mode.*
